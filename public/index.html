FAQ

Your Questions Answered

How do I book Adam Royal for an event or production?

Send us the enquiry form on this page with your date, location and a short brief. Our team will reply within one business day to arrange a call and prepare a proposal.

Do you work with both corporate and private clients?

Yes. We plan and deliver corporate events, conferences and launches as well as private parties, celebrations and red-carpet occasions.

Can one team handle the event, the filming and the marketing?

Yes. Our events, media production and marketing divisions work together, so a single brief can cover the event, the video content and the campaign around it.

How far in advance should we contact you?

As early as possible. Larger events and productions need more planning time, so get in touch as soon as you have a date in mind and we will advise on timelines.
